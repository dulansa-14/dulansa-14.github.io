About Me

Get to know more about my academic journey and interests.

I am a first-year Biotechnology undergraduate at Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT). I have a strong interest in understanding biological systems and exploring how biotechnology can contribute to scientific and technological development.

My academic interests include molecular biology, genetics, bioinformatics and the application of computational approaches to biological research. I am particularly interested in learning how biological data can be analysed to support scientific discoveries.

Through university assignments, presentations, laboratory practicals and academic projects, I am developing my scientific knowledge, communication, teamwork and problem-solving skills.

My personal approach: I believe in continuous learning, curiosity, responsibility and developing my knowledge step by step.

Learning Reflection

Reflecting on my learning experiences and personal development as a biotechnology undergraduate.

As a first-year Biotechnology undergraduate, I am beginning to understand the importance of combining scientific knowledge with practical skills, communication, and independent learning. My university activities have introduced me to different areas of biotechnology and their real-world applications.

Preparing assignments and presentations has helped me develop my research, organisation, teamwork, and communication skills. Laboratory practicals have also helped me appreciate the importance of accuracy, following instructions, safety and recording observations carefully.

These learning experiences have shown me that developing scientific knowledge takes time, practice, and continuous improvement. I recognise the importance of improving my presentation techniques, digital skills and confidence when explaining scientific concepts.

In the future, I hope to explore bioinformatics and computational biology further. I plan to continue learning, participate actively in academic activities and use feedback to improve my performance. This portfolio represents my current progress and my commitment to becoming a knowledgeable and responsible biotechnology professional.
